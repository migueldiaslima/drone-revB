How the holder attaches

  1. Pad. The spine ends in a flat pad in the keel underside (z = 0), 13.5 x 10 mm, with an M2 heat-set insert and a 2 mm locating hole.
  2. Antenna into the holder. The moulded T drops into a pocket open at the top. Each leg snaps into a TPU C-clip 3 mm from the moulding.
  3. Coax. It leaves the channel exit straight down and turns rearward into the stem with a bend radius of about 2 mm, inside the holder.
  4. Holder onto the pad. The peg goes into the locating hole, and one M2 x 8 screw from below, its head recessed in the holder, goes into the insert. Peg and screw are 4.5 mm apart, so the holder cannot rotate.
  5. Clamp. The top of the moulding bears on the pad. The T is clamped between the pocket floor and the pad, not held by the screw alone.
