How the holder attaches

  1. Pad. The spine ends in a pad in the keel underside (z = 0, 14 x 16 mm, 2.2 thick). The pad has a boss with a dia 3.2 x 4 hole for an M2 x 3 heat-set insert, pressed from below, and a dia 2 x 1.1 locating hole.
  2. Antenna into the holder. The moulded T is pushed up, stem first, between the two cheeks. It snaps past 1 mm lips under the bar. Each leg is pressed into a C-clip 3 mm from the moulding: the clip wraps 277 deg around the 1.4 mm leg and its opening is 1.06 mm, so the leg snaps in and stays.
  3. Coax. It drops straight out of the channel exit into the top of the stem. There is no bend at the moulding. The only bend is the channel's own R7.
  4. Holder onto the pad. The stem enters the slot in the top plate, the 0.9 mm peg goes into the locating hole, and one M2 x 5 screw goes in from below in front of the cradle.
  5. Clamp. The plate bears on the pad, and the bar is held between the lips and the plate. The screw only holds the holder up.
